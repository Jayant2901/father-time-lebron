Caveats

  • Values are within-season percentile ranks (0-100), not raw stats — this normalizes across eras with very different pace and scoring environments.
  • A player-season only counts if it clears a minimum minutes/games threshold; the age baseline only pools players with several qualifying seasons, to avoid short/fringe careers skewing "typical."
  • PER, WS, BPM and VORP come from Basketball-Reference and aren't available for every early season; nba_api-sourced rate stats (TS%, USG%, etc.) start in 1996-97.
  • Ages with very few historical qualifying players (shown faded/dashed) have an uncertain baseline mean — don't over-read a single outlier season there.
